## Small fix-ups to make when mining these pages for questions

1. **p54 f)** `(x+k)²−4=0 has 2 real positive roots: k>2` — wrong. Roots are −k±2; both
   positive needs **k<−2**. (Their e) −1<k<0 is right.)
2. **p26 eg2** graph labels the point (2;−10) but the working uses (−2;−10); with TP(−1;8)
   only (−2;−10) is consistent (a=−18).
3. **p41** states "AB² = yB² + xA²" as the Pythagoras rule — sloppy; true rule is
   AB² = (Δx)² + (Δy)². (Their numeric examples are fine.)
4. **p58** uses a derivative (M′=0) for max vertical distance — Grade 12 tool; the Gr11
   route is xTP=−b/2a on the difference parabola (same answer, 6,25).

## Her teaching vocabulary (use THESE in the app, not textbook phrasing)

- Parabola: **happy/sad**.
- Exponential: **taking off / landing** for b>1 vs 0<b<1; a = above/below asymptote.
- Hyperbola: a>0 decreasing / a<0 increasing (branch-wise); the points (p±√a; q±√a)
  are called the hyperbola's "TP"; distance between them = min distance between branches.
- x·f(x) inequalities via **quadrant signs** (1st/3rd vs 2nd/4th).
- Inequality method: **cut lines through every x-intercept, asymptote and (for f vs g)
  intersection, then paint + and − on each curve per section and read off.** This
  replaced the TEKENTABEL (sign table) — she changed methods when learners struggled.
  The app's quest 5 still teaches the TEKENTABEL and is therefore OUTDATED.
